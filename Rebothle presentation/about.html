About Us

Our History

Rebothle Organisation was established in 2023 by parents,social workers and therapists in Pretoria.
The initiative was born out of the lack of inclusive child development and support in the area,
where children with special needs and children without special needs were consistently separated.
Driven by the belief that every child deserves equal opportunity to play ,learn and grow.

Mision

To give every child a place of joy,warmth and belonging ,a home away from home.

Vision

To be a place where every child feels like they belong and where families find hope,support and community.
