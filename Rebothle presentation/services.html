Our Services

1. Therapy

We provide therapy for children and families to help them cope with trauma and other challenges. Our therapy services include speech therapy, occupational therapy, and family therapy.

2. Social Workers

Provides counseling and assist with community resoures like food programmes.

3. Recreational programmes

For all children to feel included we have cooking ,painting ,planting and etc.

4. Volunteer

You can assist with organizing events like fun days ,music festivals and etc.

5. Educational Support

We offer educational support to children with special needs, ensuring they have equal access to learning opportunities.
